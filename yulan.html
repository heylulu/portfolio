E-commerce · Proposals and final site

Yulan Home

A shop for wallpaper, fabric and home accessories, where nobody arrives knowing the product code they want. Three proposals went to the client, and the site that shipped is none of them — it takes the room-based browsing from the first, the stated service offer from the third, and puts a real catalogue behind both.

Web Retail Proposals to delivery Catalogue structure

The three proposals

Putting three genuinely different answers on the table is worth more than three variations of one. The illustrated proposal was never going to ship for a business that sells by SKU, but it is the one that made the conversation about how the site should feel rather than which shade of green to use.

The final site

Nine page designs, delivered with a written spec — type sizes, image dimensions and interaction states for each page — so the build did not have to guess.

What survives from proposal one is the room — it is how people actually shop for wallpaper, so it runs through the home page, the whole-home tool and even the basket. What survives from proposal three is that the service is stated rather than implied. The catalogue, the faceted filtering and the roll calculator on the product page are the part no proposal had, and the part the business actually needed.
